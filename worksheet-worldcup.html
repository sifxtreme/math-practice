🔑 Answer Key

For the grown-ups — worked steps included
Coach
3rd Grade
1. 32 water bottles.
9 crates × 6 bottles = 54. Then 54 − 22 drank = 32.
2. 10 stickers each.
5 packs × 8 = 40 stickers. Then 40 ÷ 4 friends = 10.
3. 235 samosas.
320 − 145 sold = 175. Then 175 + 60 made = 235.
4. 5:15.
3:00 + 1 hr 45 min = 4:45. Then 4:45 + 30 min = 5:15.
5. 6 shelves, with 2 trophies left over.
50 ÷ 8 = 6 remainder 2. He fills 6 shelves, 2 left.
4th Grade
1. 42 balls per team.
14 boxes × 18 = 252 balls. Then 252 ÷ 6 teams = 42.
2. $31 left.
$7 × 15 weeks = $105 saved. Then $105 − $46 − $28 = $31.
3. Area = 360 sq yd; 9 bags of seed.
24 × 15 = 360 square yards. Then 360 ÷ 40 = 9 bags.
4. They ate 9/12 (= 3/4); 3/12 (= 1/4) is left.
3 people × 3 slices = 9 slices = 9/12 = 3/4. Left: 12 − 9 = 3 slices = 3/12 = 1/4.
5. 6,750 tickets sold; 1,250 seats empty.
1,350 × 5 hours = 6,750 tickets. Then 8,000 − 6,750 = 1,250 empty.
