🔑 Answer Key

For the grown-ups — worked steps + what to watch for
Coach
3rd Grade — Musa (remainders)
Watch for: answering with the raw remainder ("7 r 5") instead of what the question asked. Don't correct the division — have him read the last sentence out loud again.
1. 8 cars.
47 ÷ 6 = 7 r 5. Seven full cars, 5 people still standing → round up to 8.
2. 7 full carts, 6 crates left.
62 ÷ 8 = 7 r 6. Asks for completely full → do not round up. 7 carts, 6 crates.
3. 9 cars.
75 ÷ 9 = 8 r 3. Everyone needs a seat → 9 cars, the last holding 3.
4. 6 books, 4 tickets left.
40 ÷ 6 = 6 r 4. Complete books only → 6, with 4 spare tickets.
5. 8 racks; 4 suitcases on the last one.
53 ÷ 7 = 7 r 4. All must be stored → 8 racks, last one holds 4.
4th Grade — Yusuf (unlike denominators)
Watch for: adding the bottoms (1/3 + 1/4 = 2/7). If it happens, go back to the pizza picture — you can't count pieces of different sizes. Also check he converted BOTH fractions, not just one.
1. 7/12 of the journey.
Common bottom 12. 1/3 = 4/12, 1/4 = 3/12. 4/12 + 3/12 = 7/12.
2. 11/15 full.
Common bottom 15. 2/5 = 6/15, 1/3 = 5/15. 6/15 + 5/15 = 11/15.
3. 7/8 of a mile.
8 already works. 1/2 = 4/8. 3/8 + 4/8 = 7/8. Good one for spotting you don't always multiply the bottoms.
4. 7/12 of a tank.
Common bottom 12. 5/6 = 10/12, 1/4 = 3/12. 10/12 − 3/12 = 7/12.
5. 13/15 of the book.
Common bottom 15. 2/3 = 10/15, 1/5 = 3/15. 10/15 + 3/15 = 13/15.
