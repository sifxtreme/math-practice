🔑 Answer Key

For the grown-ups — worked steps included
Coach
3rd Grade — kid1
Watch for: time it, but don't announce the clock — pressure changes what you're measuring. Note the finish time and errors in the log.
1. 9 bundles, 2 maps left.
74 ÷ 8 = 9 r 2. Complete bundles → 9, with 2 spare.
2. 10 tents; 3 campers in the last.
66 ÷ 7 = 9 r 3. Everyone needs a spot → 10 tents, last holds 3.
3. 358 postcards.
415 − 187 = 228. Then 228 + 130 = 358.
4. 68 trees.
9 × 6 = 54 cleared. Then 54 + 14 storm trees = 68.
5. 9 vans; 4 hikers in the last.
52 ÷ 6 = 8 r 4. Everyone rides → 9 vans, last carries 4.
4th Grade — kid2
Watch for: problem 4 is the hard one — it needs a subtraction of two fractions where neither bottom divides the other. If he stalls, that's fine; note it and re-teach rather than pushing.
1. 13/24 of a mile left.
Common bottom 24. 7/8 = 21/24, 1/3 = 8/24. 21/24 − 8/24 = 13/24.
2. Cleared 13/20; blocked 7/20.
Common bottom 20. 2/5 = 8/20, 1/4 = 5/20. 8/20 + 5/20 = 13/20. Blocked: 20/20 − 13/20 = 7/20.
3. 11/24 of the tank.
Common bottom 24. 5/6 = 20/24, 3/8 = 9/24. 20/24 − 9/24 = 11/24.
4. 7/12 of the park.
Common bottom 12. 3/4 = 9/12, 1/6 = 2/12. 9/12 − 2/12 = 7/12.
5. 9/10 full; 1/10 empty.
Common bottom 10. 1/2 = 5/10, 2/5 = 4/10. 5/10 + 4/10 = 9/10. Empty: 1/10.
