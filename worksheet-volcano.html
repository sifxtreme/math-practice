🔑 Answer Key

For the grown-ups — worked steps included
Coach
3rd Grade — kid1 (remainders)
Watch for: whether he's now asking himself which way to round WITHOUT being prompted. That's the skill going from Shaky to Solid.
1. 8 packs; 2 samples in the last one.
58 ÷ 8 = 7 r 2. All must come down → 8 packs, last holds 2.
2. 10 full shelves, 1 helmet left.
91 ÷ 9 = 10 r 1. Completely full only → 10 shelves, 1 helmet over.
3. 280 rocks.
342 − 158 = 184. Then 184 + 96 = 280.
4. 37 panes whole.
7 × 8 = 56 panes. 56 − 19 cracked = 37.
5. 10 cable cars; 1 person in the last.
46 ÷ 5 = 9 r 1. Everyone rides → 10 cars, last carries 1.
4th Grade — kid2 (unlike denominators)
Watch for: the subtraction ones (3, 4, 5). Same method, and that's the point — if he can add but not subtract, he's memorised a procedure rather than understood common denominators.
1. Covered 7/8; 1/8 still clear.
Common bottom 8. 3/8 + 4/8 = 7/8. Clear: 8/8 − 7/8 = 1/8.
2. 5/6 of a mile.
Common bottom 6. 2/3 = 4/6. 4/6 + 1/6 = 5/6.
3. 9/20 of the battery.
Common bottom 20. 7/10 = 14/20, 1/4 = 5/20. 14/20 − 5/20 = 9/20.
4. 7/15 of a mile left.
Common bottom 15. 4/5 = 12/15, 1/3 = 5/15. 12/15 − 5/15 = 7/15.
5. 7/12 of the tube.
Common bottom 12. 5/6 = 10/12, 1/4 = 3/12. 10/12 − 3/12 = 7/12.
