1.
A win = 3 points, a tie = 1 point, a loss = 0 points. The Falcons played 3 games: 1 win, 1 tie, and 1 loss. The Hawks played 3 games: 2 wins and 1 loss. How many points does each team have? Who has more, and by how many?
SHOW YOUR THINKING
Answer:
Did you know? Teams are ranked in their group by points, so a win (3 points) is worth much more than a tie (1 point).
2.
Three teammates — Yusuf, Zaid, and Hamza — play three positions: forward, goalie, and defender. Clue 1: Yusuf is not the goalie. Clue 2: Hamza is the defender. Which position does each player play?
SHOW YOUR THINKING
Answer:
Did you know? The goalie is the only player allowed to touch the ball with their hands — and only inside their own box.
3.
A knockout tournament starts with 16 teams. In each round, half the teams are knocked out and half move on. How many rounds does it take to reach 1 champion? How many games are played in total?
SHOW YOUR THINKING
Answer:
Did you know? In a knockout tournament, the total number of games is always one less than the number of teams (16 teams → 15 games).
4.
Two brothers scored goals all tournament. Together they scored 20 goals. Yusuf scored 4 more goals than Musa. How many goals did each brother score?
SHOW YOUR THINKING
Answer:
Did you know? The World Cup is held once every 4 years, so a player only gets a few chances in their whole life to play in one.
5.
A tournament has 12 teams, split into groups of 4. How many groups are there? In one group, each of the 4 teams plays every other team exactly once. How many games are played in that group? (Hint: list the matchups.)
SHOW YOUR THINKING
Answer:
Did you know? The real 2026 World Cup is split into exactly 12 groups of 4 teams — just like this puzzle.

🔑 Answer Key

For the grown-ups — reasoning included
Coach
3rd Grade
1. Musa scored 3 goals; Musa is the middle.
9 total − 5 (Idris) − 1 (Omar) = 3 for Musa. Ordered: Omar 1, Musa 3, Idris 5 → Musa is in the middle.
2. 15 and 18. Rule: add 3 each time.
3, 6, 9, 12 each go up by 3. 12 + 3 = 15, 15 + 3 = 18.
3. Scored 6, missed 3.
The two numbers add to 9 and differ by 3. Guess & check: 6 + 3 = 9 and 6 − 3 = 3. So scored 6, missed 3.
4. Red, Blue, Green, Yellow.
Red is 1st and Yellow is 4th. That leaves 2nd and 3rd for Blue and Green; Blue is higher, so Blue 2nd, Green 3rd. Order: Red, Blue, Green, Yellow.
5. 6 uniforms.
3 jersey colors × 2 shorts colors = 6. (red-black, red-white, blue-black, blue-white, white-black, white-white.)
4th Grade
1. Falcons 4, Hawks 6. Hawks have 2 more.
Falcons: 3 + 1 + 0 = 4. Hawks: 3 + 3 + 0 = 6. 6 − 4 = 2 more for the Hawks.
2. Hamza = defender, Yusuf = forward, Zaid = goalie.
Hamza is the defender (Clue 2). Yusuf is not the goalie (Clue 1), so Yusuf is the forward. That leaves goalie for Zaid.
3. 4 rounds; 15 games total.
16 → 8 → 4 → 2 → 1 is 4 rounds. Games: 8 + 4 + 2 + 1 = 15 (or 16 − 1 = 15).
4. Musa 8 goals, Yusuf 12 goals.
Take the 4 extra off the 20: 20 − 4 = 16, then 16 ÷ 2 = 8 for Musa. Yusuf = 8 + 4 = 12. Check: 8 + 12 = 20. ✓
5. 3 groups; 6 games per group.
12 ÷ 4 = 3 groups. In a group of 4 (call them A, B, C, D): AB, AC, AD, BC, BD, CD = 6 games.
