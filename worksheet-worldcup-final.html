🔑 Answer Key

For the grown-ups — worked steps included
Coach
3rd Grade
1. 45 seats filled.
7 rows × 9 seats = 63. Then 63 − 18 empty = 45.
2. 8 juice boxes per team.
48 − 8 to coaches = 40. Then 40 ÷ 5 teams = 8.
3. 211 scarves.
268 − 132 sold = 136. Then 136 + 75 delivered = 211.
4. 1:30 p.m.
45 + 15 + 45 = 105 minutes = 1 hr 45 min. Then 11:45 a.m. + 1 hr 45 min = 1:30 p.m.
5. 6 full vans, 3 players left over.
45 ÷ 7 = 6 remainder 3. 6 vans full, 3 players left (so they'd need a 7th van).
4th Grade
1. 48 medals per team.
16 crates × 24 medals = 384. Then 384 ÷ 8 teams = 48.
2. $29 left.
$9 × 13 weeks = $117 saved. Then $117 − $65 − $23 = $29.
3. Area = 576 sq yd; 12 rolls of turf.
32 × 18 = 576 square yards. Then 576 ÷ 48 = 12 rolls.
4. They ate 12/16 = 3/4; 4/16 = 1/4 is left.
4 cousins × 3 portions = 12 portions. 12/16 simplifies to 3/4. Left: 16 − 12 = 4, so 4/16 = 1/4.
5. 9,800 jerseys sold; 2,200 left.
2,450 × 4 days = 9,800 sold. Then 12,000 − 9,800 = 2,200 left.
