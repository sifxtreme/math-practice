Did you know? Desert guides navigated by the stars. The North Star sits almost still in the sky all night, so it always points the same way.

SHOW YOUR WORK

Did you know? For hundreds of years only China knew how to make silk. The secret was that it comes from the cocoon of a caterpillar.

SHOW YOUR WORK
Answer:
Did you know? Travellers carried water in bags made of animal skin. The water seeped through very slowly, and that tiny bit of evaporation kept the rest cool.

SHOW YOUR WORK
Answer:
Did you know? A hand-knotted carpet can hold over a million knots. A weaver ties every single one by hand.

Did you know? Glass beads were once so valuable along these routes that they were traded like money.

On the first day a caravan covers 1/3 of the route. On the second day it covers another 2/5 of the route. What fraction of the whole route has it covered so far?
SHOW YOUR WORK
Answer:
Did you know? Very few traders walked the whole road. Goods were handed from caravan to caravan, so a bolt of silk might change owners a dozen times.

Answer:
Did you know? Saffron is still the most expensive spice on earth. It takes around 75,000 flowers to make one pound of it.

Did you know? A camel's hump stores fat, not water. It can drink 100 litres in about ten minutes when it finally reaches a well.

🔑 Answer Key

Silk Road Caravan Math — worked steps for the grown-up
Adult
kid1 — 3rd Grade
1. 640 kilograms
145 × 6 = 870 kg loaded · 870 − 230 = 640 kg. Watch for: subtracting 230 from 145 first.
2. 8 guides
57 ÷ 8 = 7 remainder 1. Seven guides cover 56 traders; 1 trader is still waiting, so an 8th guide is needed. The remainder is the whole point — "7" is the trap answer.
3. 45 bolts
72 ÷ 8 = 9 bolts per crate · 3 × 9 = 27 sold · 72 − 27 = 45 bolts. Three operations; the order is the test.
4. kid2's
1/2 = 4/8. Compare 4/8 with 3/8 → 4/8 is larger, so kid2's has more. Watch for: picking Hamza because 3 and 8 are bigger numbers than 1 and 2.
5. 12 feet
Area = length × width, so 96 ÷ 8 = 12 feet. Watch for: subtracting (96 − 8) instead of dividing.
kid2 — 4th Grade
1. 972 miles
34 × 26 = 884 (34 × 20 = 680, 34 × 6 = 204, 680 + 204 = 884) · 884 + 88 = 972 miles.
2. 208 per box, 6 in the pouch
1,462 ÷ 7 = 208 remainder 6. Check: 7 × 208 = 1,456, and 1,462 − 1,456 = 6. Both numbers are part of the answer.
3. 11/15 of the route
Common denominator 15: 1/3 = 5/15 and 2/5 = 6/15 · 5/15 + 6/15 = 11/15. Watch for: 3/8 — adding the tops and the bottoms straight across.
4. 5.25 silver coins
Compare first: 4.75 < 4.80, so the cheaper stall is the 4.75 one · 10 − 4.75 = 5.25. Watch for: reading 4.8 as smaller than 4.75 because it has fewer digits.
5. 13 full days, 32 litres left over
9 × 4 = 36 litres per day · 500 ÷ 36 = 13 remainder 32. Check: 36 × 13 = 468, and 500 − 468 = 32. Watch for: answering 13.8 days — the question asks for full days.
