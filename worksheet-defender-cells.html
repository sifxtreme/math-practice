SHOW YOUR THINKING
Answer:
Did you know? A single drop of blood holds thousands of defender cells, all patrolling for anything that does not belong.

SHOW YOUR THINKING
Answer:
Did you know? When you rest, your body spends more energy on defence. That is one reason sleep helps you get better faster.
4.

SHOW YOUR THINKING
Answer:
Did you know? Antibodies are tiny Y-shaped tools your defender cells make, each one built to grab a single kind of germ.
5.

SHOW YOUR THINKING
Answer:
Did you know? Once your body beats a germ, it keeps a memory of it. Meet the same germ again and the defence is ready in hours, not days.

🔑 Answer Key

For the grown-ups — worked steps included
Coach
3rd Grade — Musa
1. 601 defender cells left.
115 × 6 = 690, then 690 − 89 = 601. Multiply first, subtract second.
2. 11 full trays, 6 left over — but 12 trays to hold them all.
94 ÷ 8 = 11 remainder 6. The 6 spares still need a tray, so 12. "11" is one step short.
3. 72 beds.
4 × 15 = 60, then 60 + 12 = 72. Multiply first, then add the last room.
4. The 3/4 bottle.
Eighths: 3/4 = 6/8 vs 5/8, so 3/4 holds more — but only once the eighths make them fair to compare.
5. Other side 9 cm; perimeter 34 cm.
72 ÷ 8 = 9 cm (the missing side). Perimeter = 2 × (8 + 9) = 34 cm. Gives area, asks perimeter — reverse of yesterday.
Watch for: Day four on missing sides, now clued by area, not perimeter — #5 needs a divide, then the perimeter. Halving 72 (yesterday's move) is the slip. #2: expect "11 trays". #4: he may still pick the bigger top number.
4th Grade — Yusuf
1. 11/15 of the tube.
Fifteenths: 2/5 = 6/15, 1/3 = 5/15, so 6/15 + 5/15 = 11/15. Both had to change — neither was already 15.
2. 13/24 of the store.
Twenty-fourths: 7/8 = 21/24, 1/3 = 8/24. 21/24 − 8/24 = 13/24. No scaffold, and a subtraction.
3. 864 wells.
24 × 36 = (24 × 30) + (24 × 6) = 720 + 144 = 864.
4. $14.25 spent; $5.75 change.
4.75 × 3 = 14.25, then 20.00 − 14.25 = 5.75. Pad the 20 as 20.00 and line up the points.
5. 2,196 people — 274 complete folders, 4 left over.
Day first: 1,464 ÷ 6 = 244. Then 244 × 9 = 2,196. Then 2,196 ÷ 8 = 274 r 4. Check: 274 × 8 + 4 = 2,196. ✓
Watch for: Third and last guided day on unlike denominators — #1 keeps a light scaffold, #2 has none. If both come back clean with work shown, the guided slot moves to decimals tomorrow. #2 straight across gives 6/5 — ask if a store can be more than full. #5: jumping to 1,464 × 9 skips the daily rate.
